Builders First

We tested three pre-registered claims about NEAR's developer base. One holds, barely; two fail. The surprise: almost every hackathon builder is new to NEAR (97%), and at least a third of their repos show AI co-authorship (34%), but developers follow usage rather than lead it.

Verdict: Neutral (1 of 3 claims hold)

Data as of: NEAR deployments to 2026-11 · GH Archive reliable to 2026-02 · hackathon data fetched 2026-11-12 · Open Dev Data commit d664cfa. Every number on this page is read from data/cache/claims.json.

Headline findings

  • 97% of NEAR HACKATHON builders had no prior public NEAR activity (310 of 319 commit authors across 367 GitHub repos). Caveat: "NEAR activity" means pushes or PRs on NEAR repos listed in Open Dev Data, so builders who only worked in unlisted repos count as new; this overstates newness.
  • ≥41% of hackathon commits are AI co-authored (6,604 of 15,960 non-bot commits; 34% of repos have at least one). A lower bound: trailers are easy to strip, and this repo strips its own.
  • Developers do not lead NEAR usage (C2 killed: best p = 0.091 raw, Bonferroni threshold 0.0083); the reverse direction is suggestive (p ≈ 0.01–0.03 at lags 2–6) but not significant after Bonferroni (threshold 0.0083): usage may pull developers in, not the other way round.
  • Output per developer fell to 0.56–0.67× its 2023–24 level (contracts deployed per monthly active developer, coverage-adjusted and raw).

What would change the verdict

  • C1 (HOLDS) flips to KILLED if NEAR's 6-month retention drops below the peer median (today 12.04% vs 12.03%, a margin of +0.01 pp). Watch it: the margin is inside sampling error.
  • C2 (KILLED) flips to HOLDS if any lag 1–6 reaches p < 0.0083 for deploys or unique deployers, on both raw and coverage-adjusted MAD (on only one: FRAGILE). Best today: 0.091 raw, 0.124 adjusted.
  • C3a (KILLED) flips to HOLDS if mean contracts per MAD from 2025-01 exceeds 1.10× the 2023–24 mean: above 1.66 raw (today 1.01) and above 1.66 adjusted (today 0.84).
  • C3b (HOLDS) flips to KILLED if new-to-NEAR authors fall to 50% or below in a future cohort (today 97.2%, stricter of the two shares).
  • Verdict (Neutral) moves to Bull with one more HOLDS (C2, or C3a while C3b holds), and to Bear if C1 flips.

Exploratory, not pre-registered

  • Deploys → developers (the reverse of C2, raw MAD, log-differenced): Granger p-values lag 1: 0.948, lag 2: 0.012, lag 3: 0.021, lag 4: 0.014, lag 5: 0.026, lag 6: 0.022. Bonferroni threshold across 6 lags: 0.0083. No lag survives the correction, so this is a hint, not a finding. It was not pre-registered and has no effect on the verdict.
  • C2 out-of-sample check (fit to 2024-12, one-step forecasts after): adding lagged MAD changes forecast RMSE by a factor of 1.36 for deploys and 1.03 for unique deployers (below 1 would mean developers help predict usage).

Bear case

  • NEAR's monthly active developers peaked at 1,179 in 2022-04 and were 484 in 2026-02, the last month with reliable GH Archive coverage.
  • Developers do not lead usage (C2 killed). If anything, the exploratory reverse test suggests usage pulls developers in, which makes developer data a lagging, not leading, signal for the token.
  • Output per developer fell (0.67× raw, 0.56× adjusted), so fewer developers are not being offset by more contracts each.
  • NEAR's retention is no better than peers' (a statistical tie at 6 months; behind at 12 months).
  • The hackathon brought 319 mostly new authors, but a two-week cohort says nothing yet about whether they stay; C1 suggests roughly 12% of new NEAR developers are still active six months later.

Limitations

  • GH Archive volume breaks in 2025-06 (pushes fall about 30%) and collapses after 2026-02 (pull requests, then pushes). Series end at 2026-02; C2 and C3a are also run on coverage-adjusted MAD, and disagreements would be reported as FRAGILE.
  • Developer identity is the GH Archive actor of a push or PR on a repo listed in Open Dev Data. Electric Capital counts original commit authors, so levels differ from the Developer Report. Private repos and unlisted repos are invisible.
  • History starts 2021-01 (budget), so some 2022 "new" developers are returning ones.
  • Deployments are transaction-level DEPLOY_CONTRACT only; factory-created contracts are excluded on purpose (see THESIS.md methodology, item 4).
  • C3b covers 367 repos with a GitHub link out of 678 nearhunt.xyz projects (764 official submissions); 1,500 commits had no linked GitHub account. 16 authors hit GitHub's 300-event feed cap; 11 of them have feeds that stop after 2026-10-19 and are shown both included and excluded.
  • AI co-authorship is detected from commit trailers only, a lower bound.
  • The C3a threshold (>10%) was fixed after the deployment series had been seen; see THESIS.md methodology item 6 for the exact order of events with commit hashes.
